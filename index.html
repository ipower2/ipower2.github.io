Our Story

From one small textile shop to a house of silk.

Kumba Handloom House began in the early 1980s as a small textile shop. Over the decades it earned its customers' trust the slow way — one family, one wedding, one recommendation at a time — and grew into the showroom it is today.

The 1980s

A small textile shop

Fabrics sold the old way: measured, folded and handed over the counter, with a reputation built entirely on word of mouth.

Growing years

Into silk & cotton sarees

The shop widened into bridal silks, soft silk and cotton handloom sarees — and slowly became the place families came to for the important occasions.

The Silk Notebook

Four things worth knowing before you buy silk.

Small pieces of advice we give across the counter every week — so you can judge a saree as carefully as we do.

01

How to read real zari

Turn the pallu over. Genuine zari carries the same woven pattern on the reverse, not a printed imitation. Ask us and we will show you the difference side by side.

02

Match the blouse first

Bring the blouse colour you have in mind, or pick it with the saree in hand. A contrast blouse changes the whole drape — it is worth the extra ten minutes.

03

Storing silk well

Wrap silk in a soft cotton cloth rather than plastic, refold along a different line each season, and keep it away from direct sunlight and naphthalene.

04

The first wash

Dry-clean the first time, and dry-clean thereafter. Never wring silk, and never let it soak — the weave and the zari both suffer for it.

Can I see the sarees before I travel to the showroom?
Yes. Send us a photo or a description on WhatsApp and we will tell you what is in stock this week, along with the range and the price band. It saves you a wasted trip.
Do you take bridal appointments?
We do. Tell us the wedding date and roughly when you would like to come, and we will keep the showroom quiet and unhurried for you. Bridal shopping is easier when nobody is waiting.
What are your store timings?
Monday to Saturday, 10:00 am to 8:30 pm. Sunday, 11:00 am to 1:30 pm. Timings can shift on festival days, so a quick call before you travel is always best — +91 97901 15883.
How do I know the zari is genuine?
Ask us in the showroom and we will show you the weave and the reverse of the pallu. Real zari carries the same woven pattern on both sides; imitation zari is printed and stops at the surface.
Where exactly are you located?
